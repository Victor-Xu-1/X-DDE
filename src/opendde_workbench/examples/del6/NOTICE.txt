Public scientific example: UNC DEL006 / BRD4. Original target remains BRD4; this is not a STAT6 experiment.
Source repository: https://github.com/Popov-Lab-UNC/DELi/tree/edeb06ca8ffe6a158c1231c4014e569e04a9b83b/examples
Scientific citation: Wellnitz et al., Open-Source DNA-Encoded Library Informatics Package for Design, Decoding, and Analysis: DELi. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-026-01296-1 ; public preprint https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11888370/
The count projection retains all 518,285 observed rows, original order, compound/cycle identities, supplied SMILES and eight raw integer sample counts. No unobserved members or measurements were invented. The 1,000 public raw reads demonstrate decoding only and are not the full eight-sample experiment.
The follow-up CSV is a qualitative transcription of the upstream reported validation, not a new experiment or an invented numerical KD. Chemical enumeration and explicit off-DNA capping are computational hypotheses.

The linked scientific preprint is CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Source input and qualitative follow-up transformations are described above; no exact experimental KD has been transcribed.
